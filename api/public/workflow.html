1 · Onboard

The owner signs in and describes the business once. The system classifies its weather sensitivity and warms the climate data for its location in the background.

  1. 01

    Sign in with Google

    OwnerBank officer

    You do

    GET/auth/config POST/auth/google

    System does

    Checks the Google token, opens a session cookie, and gives the role: bank for addresses listed in AUTH_BANK_EMAILS / AUTH_BANK_DOMAINS, otherwise owner.

    You see

    GET/auth/me

    Your name and role. Every route below needs this session.

  2. 02

    Register the business

    Owner

    You do

    POST/businesses

    Profile, primary location, operations, suppliers, contacts and consent, in one form.

    System does

    • Refuses it unless consent.data_processing is true.
    • Queues the weather-impact classifier.
    • Warms the climate cell with 15 years of history (step 4).

    You see

    The stored business with scoring: "pending" until the background work finishes.

  3. 03

    Add a financial snapshot

    Owner

    You do

    POST/businesses/{id}/snapshots

    6 to 12 months of revenue, monthly costs, existing EMIs, debt and cash.

    System does

    Derives average revenue, volatility, operating margin, DSCR and cash buffer. Each snapshot is a new version; old ones are kept.

    You see

    GET/businesses/{id}/snapshots
  4. 04

    Classify weather impact and warm climate data

    Automatic

    Triggered by

    Steps 2 and 3, and any change of location.

    System does

    • Rates how each of six hazards hits this business (physical loss, demand change). Sector defaults when no OpenAI key is set.
    • Screens the location's climate cell on 15 years of history and caches it.
    • A failed call stays pending and is retried.

    You see

    GET/businesses/{id}/impact-profile

2 · Assess

Before a loan, the owner can ask the advisor whether it is affordable, and the bank sees a baseline and a climate-adjusted score.

  1. 05

    Check climate risk at a location

    OwnerBank officer

    You do

    POST/climate/profile GET/climate/events

    Or use the map demo.

    System does

    Snaps the point to grid cells, screens six hazards, adds the 7-day forecast and scores each hazard 0–1.

    You see

    A grade A–E, a score per hazard with a reason, and any active forecast events.

  2. 06

    Ask the advisor before borrowing

    Owner

    You do

    GET/advisor/businesses POST/advisor/chat

    "Should I take a ₹3L loan?"

    System does

    Reads your snapshot, loans, weather impact, climate profile and finance score; asks up to two questions it cannot answer from your data; runs the numbers in code, never in the model.

    You see

    A verdict (viable / with conditions / not advisable) with cited figures.

    GET/advisor/conversations/{businessId}
  3. 07

    Review the finance score

    Bank officer

    You do

    GET/finance/business/{id}/profile POST/finance/business/{id}/scenario POST/finance/business/{id}/repayment-capacity POST/businesses/{id}/snapshots/{v}/verify

    System does

    Scores financial health, stress-tests it against each relevant hazard, and caps the climate penalty. The bank can mark a snapshot as verified.

    You see

    Baseline and climate-adjusted scores with their drivers, a what-if result, and the largest EMI the business can carry under stress.

3 · Lend

The bank records the loan and moves it forward. From "authorised" on, the business is monitored and its key details are locked to the bank.

  1. 08

    Record the loan

    Bank officer

    You do

    POST/businesses/{id}/loans

    System does

    Refuses it unless the owner agreed to share with the bank (consent.share_with_bank). The loan starts as applied; the advisor sees it as PRE_LOAN.

    You see

    The loan with its EMI, added to the business's debt service.

  2. 09

    Authorise, then mark active

    Bank officer

    You do

    PATCH/loans/{id}/status

    applied → authorised → active, one step at a time.

    System does

    • authorised: stores the first finance profile, locks the owner's key details, and the monitor starts watching the location.
    • active: the advisor moves the loan to ACTIVE.

    You see

    GET/advisor/loans/{loanId}/state

4 · Watch & alert

No one has to do anything here. A separate monitor process watches every live loan and the alert worker emails people who agreed to it.

  1. 10

    The monitor checks each location

    Automatic

    Runs as

    npm run monitor, a process of its own, every 15 minutes.

    System does

    • Watches every business with an authorised or active loan.
    • Checks a cell every 6 h, more often when the forecast worsens.
    • Heavy rain, heat, wind and river thresholds give WATCH, WARNING or SEVERE.

    You see

    GET/climate/cell/{cellId}

    When a level rises, an alert is queued (step 11).

  2. 11

    Alert emails go out

    AutomaticOwner receives

    Runs as

    npm run alerts:worker. In dry_run mode (the default) emails are logged, not sent.

    System does

    • Owner email in English, Hindi or Marathi, with preventive steps — only with consent and the email channel chosen.
    • Bank email with the evidence; WATCH alerts wait for a 09:00 digest.
    • The same hazard is not repeated within 6 hours unless it gets worse.

    You see

    An email in your inbox with what is coming and what to do before it.

  3. 12

    See every alert

    Bank officer

    You do

    GET/alerts GET/alerts?status=sent

    System does

    Lists each alert with its status (sent, suppressed and why, failed) and the message as rendered. Recipients are never shown.

    You see

    Which borrowers were warned, about what, and when.

5 · Advise & recover

After an alert the advisor helps the owner act; if damage happens it proposes repayment relief, and only the bank can approve it.

  1. 13

    The advisor follows up the alert

    AutomaticOwner receives

    Triggered by

    An owner alert email that was actually sent (checked every 60 s). Consent and cooldown have already been applied.

    System does

    Moves each live loan to ALERT and sends ranked actions with deadlines in IST. It goes back to ACTIVE once the window ends.

    You see

    "1. Move stock off the floor by Thu 6 pm …", and a chat you can reply in via POST/advisor/chat.

  2. 14

    Report damage

    Owner

    You do

    POST/advisor/damage-report

    Days closed, stock and equipment lost, reopening date, insurance claim. Or tell the advisor in chat.

    System does

    Moves the loan to IMPACTED, recomputes 12 months of cash flow, and compares moratorium, tenure extension, step-down EMI and insurance bridge.

    You see

    The recommended option and a proposal marked pending bank. Nothing is approved by the advisor.

  3. 15

    Decide on the relief, then close recovery

    Bank officer

    You do

    POST/advisor/proposals/{id}/decision POST/advisor/loans/{loanId}/close-recovery

    System does

    accept moves the loan to RECOVERY and records who decided. Closing recovery returns it to ACTIVE.

    You see

    GET/advisor/loans/{loanId}/state

    The state with its full history.

  4. 16

    Check whether the alert was right

    Automatic

    Runs as

    npm run monitor:verify, once a day.

    System does

    Five days after an event window, compares the forecast with observed weather and labels it hit, false alarm or missed.

    You see

    Labelled events that become training data for the future ML model.

Rules the system enforces

  • You only see your own business. Owners see what they created; the bank sees what owners agreed to share. Anything else answers "not found".
  • No message without consent. Owners are emailed only if they agreed to alerts and chose email; an opt-out always wins.
  • No repeated alerts. One email per hazard per 6 hours, unless the severity rises.
  • The advisor never approves. It can only propose relief; a bank officer accepts or rejects it.
  • Locked after authorisation. Once a loan is authorised, only the bank can change the business's key details.
  • Every write is audited and can be retried safely. Send an Idempotency-Key header; the same key never applies a change twice.

Endpoint reference

Green GET links open the live answer in a new tab, using your current session. Writes need a JSON body, so they are listed with an example; send them from the app, curl or an API client, signed in, with an Idempotency-Key. {id} means a real id from an earlier answer.
