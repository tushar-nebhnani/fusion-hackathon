Climate-adjusted credit risk · India

One loan, from application to recovery.

This page follows a single business through every service: it applies, its weather sensitivity is classified, its location is scored for six hazards, the bank scores and authorises the loan, the monitor watches its cell, and the advisor helps when an event hits.

03

How weather moves this business

A classifier reads the description and gives two values per hazard: physical (how much of the business stops) and demand (how customers react). Rules bound it: a physical floor, sector defaults at low confidence, a full fallback if the model fails.

06

The bank records a loan and scores it

Rules decide; the model only informs. The finance service scores six sub-scores, stress-tests the business against each hazard in its cell, and reports a climate-adjusted score with the driver behind every point. Good weather adds at most 3 points.

07

The bank authorises; the monitor starts watching

Authorising stores the first finance profile, locks the risk fields against the owner, and puts the business's cell on the monitor's watch list. The monitor only queues alerts; a separate worker sends them after consent and cooldown checks.

08

An event hits; the advisor helps

The advisor follows the loan through three modes: pre-loan advice, alert guidance, and a recovery plan after damage. Every number in an answer must match a tool result. It can only propose a repayment change; a bank officer decides. Talk to it in the chat panel.

The advisor reads the same business and loan as the steps above, from the input service, and follows the same access rule. Until a business is created here, the chat uses SME-0142, one of four demo businesses from api/data/smes.json that stay available outside production.

Audit trailaudit_events
  • One row for each write, each refusal (401 or 403) and each failed attempt to open a business.
  • Request bodies, tokens and financial values are never stored.
  • Append only: a database trigger refuses UPDATE and DELETE. No endpoint reads it; query the database with the X-Request-Id from the wire log.
Safety limits that stay fixedBy design
  • Climate agent: may raise a hazard's category, never lower it.
  • Finance: good weather adds at most 3 points.
  • Alerts: consent and cooldown are checked before anything is sent.
  • Advisor: a verdict can be stricter than the numbers imply, never more lenient.
